## The assessment

The baseline answered "how is this project faring" and left "what is this project"
to a mechanism table and a release list. That was the wrong proportion for a report
meant to show the forest, and this one corrects it.

What the correction reveals is that **gzkit is a substantially larger and more
coherent built system than the baseline conveyed** — 61 CLI verbs, 97 registered
validator scopes, a 240-file distribution contract enforced in both directions, and
a two-vocabulary layer model that is genuinely load-bearing rather than decorative.
The baseline said "the architecture is coherent." Having now read it rather than
summarised it, the stronger claim holds: the design is unusually well-specified, and
its documents are honest about their own gaps in ways most project documentation is
not.

It also reveals a fault line the baseline caught one instance of and treated as an
anecdote. The airlock's empty seam-map is not a one-off. It is one of **at least
four** mechanisms that are built, tested, registered, and structurally unable to
do their job because nothing populates their input. The recurring shape is precise
enough to name: **a declared-but-unwired parameter with a permissive default.** A
default of `()`, `0`, `None` or `lambda: []` keeps every signature valid, every type
check green and every test passing, while making the mechanism vacuous. gzkit's
witnesses ask whether a mechanism is present. None of them asks whether its input
is ever non-empty.

That is the finding. It is narrower and more actionable than the baseline's
"defect-absorption equilibrium," and it sits underneath it.

## What gzkit is

gzkit is a **governance meta-harness**. The boundary is stated precisely in the
README and it is the first thing to understand about the product:

> "It does not run agents: a vendor harness turns a model into an agent, and gzkit
> operates one tier up, wrapping agent harnesses such as Codex, Claude Code,
> Copilot, and adjacent tools with deterministic governance surfaces, evidence
> capture, gate enforcement, and human attestation."

The problem it exists for is stated in the PRD, and it is a problem about the human
rather than the agent:

> "AI-assisted development creates a drift problem: humans approve work they didn't
> verify, attest to artifacts they didn't read, and gradually become rubber stamps
> for agent-generated output. Existing tools either ignore governance entirely or
> bury it in ceremony that agents route around."
>
> "gzkit exists to keep the human at index zero — first in priority, final in
> authority."

The organising metaphor is constitutional: humans are the **Legislature** (intent,
constraints, final judgment), agents the **Executive** (execution within bounds,
evidence generation), tests and BDD the **Judiciary** (verification). Three concerns
compose it, with a stated dependency: *"Specification without governance drifts.
Governance without methodology is theater. Methodology without specification is
arbitrary."*

The design record is candid about the cost, which is rarer than the claim itself.
The rendering-substrate doctrine says of its own weight: *"the substrate is heavier
than direct hand-authoring; that weight is the product, not overhead."* And it is
honest about the limits of its reach: *"The substrate's resilience is exactly that
it does not depend on runtime control of the vendor harness. Composition happens
between turns; rendering happens before the agent loads; loading is the vendor
harness's call."*

## What it does

This is the section the baseline lacked. A reader should be able to finish it able
to say what a user actually gets.

### The command surface

**61 top-level verbs**, each with a uniform exit-code contract (`0` success,
`1` user/config error, `2` usage or IO, `3` policy breach). Grouped by what they
are for — the grouping is mine; the CLI prints one flat list:

| Family | Verbs | What the operator does with it |
|---|---:|---|
| Adoption | 2 | `init` scaffolds governance into a project; `upgrade` refreshes surfaces from the wheel |
| Governance spine | 13 | Author and move artifacts along `prd → constitute → plan → specify → obpi → task` |
| Verification & evidence | 17 | `check`, `test`, `validate`, `gates`, `covers`, `arb` — run the gates and emit receipts |
| Derived views | 10 | `status`, `state`, `report`, `ontology` — read what happened without changing it |
| Content & control surfaces | 6 | `content`, `agent`, `skill`, `personas` — author canon and regenerate mirrors |
| Operations | 13 | `mx`, `airlock`, `chores`, `git-sync`, `handoff`, `insights`, `issue` |

The verbs that carry the most weight for a working operator: `gz check` runs a
62-step quality sequence; `gz obpi complete` atomically validates, writes evidence,
flips status and emits a receipt; `gz arb` wraps QA commands and emits
schema-validated JSON receipts that are the canonical attestation evidence;
`gz content compose` and `gz content commit` stage and land control-surface
renditions under attestation; `gz agent sync control-surfaces` regenerates every
vendor mirror from canon in one invocation.

### What an adopter receives

`pip install py-gzkit && gz init` delivers a **frozen 240-file distribution
contract** across five canonical surface kinds:

| Surface | Files delivered |
|---|---:|
| Chores | 125 (across 39 chore slugs) |
| Skills | 70 |
| Rules | 26 |
| Templates | 12 |
| Personas | 7 |
| **Total** | **240** |

It is enforced in both directions — three declared drift classes
(`ON_DISK_NOT_INCLUDED`, `BASELINE_NOT_ON_DISK`, `ON_DISK_NOT_BASELINE`) — and the
trust doctrine explains why this sits *upstream* of everything else: *"if a
canonical surface only exists in this repo's `.gzkit/` and never ships, then T1
(canon-as-truth) is silently project-specific instead of project-portable."* The
sharpest statement of the obligation: *"a wheel that ships without a canonical
surface is a T0 breach, regardless of whether downstream `gz init` reports
success."*

This repository's own working set is larger than what it ships: 72 skills, 25
rules, 40 registered chores, 7 personas, 12 templates. The two extra skills are
declared `project_local` and are absent from the wheel deliberately.

### The verification surface

10,541 unit tests pass in 91.5 seconds. `gz check` runs 62 steps and exits 0. There
are **97 registered validator scopes**, 65.8% REQ coverage (1,794 of 2,728), 467 BDD
scenarios, and 16,973 ledger events across 67 event types. The chore registry
carries 40 entries, each declaring a `class` and a `rung` — 26 conformance, 5
coherence, 4 mining, 3 curation, 2 currency; 18 authorised to repair, 16 to propose,
6 operator-only.

## How the parts compose

gzkit uses **two orthogonal layer vocabularies**, and keeping them distinct is the
single most important structural fact about the system.

**Storage tiers (L1/L2/L3)** answer *which layer wins?*

| | Authority | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** Canon | "Defines *what should exist*" | Git-versioned markdown and YAML |
| **L2** Ledger | "Defines *what has happened* — the runtime truth" | Append-only JSONL; lines never edited or deleted |
| **L3** Derived | "Defines *what is convenient* … never the source of truth" | Rebuildable; delete it all and it reconstructs |

Precedence is explicit: **`L2 > L1 > L3`**. Frontmatter is *"a lazy mirror"* and is
never read as source-of-truth for completion. L3 *"cannot block gates."*

**Trust tiers (T0–T3)** answer a different question the storage model does not
cover — *how does each layer verify that the layer below produced what it assumes?*
The trust doctrine names the failure class and is worth quoting because it is the
intellectual core of the project:

> "**Trust-chain poisoning.** When Layer A's output feeds Layer B's decision, and A
> is silently wrong, B's decision looks correct… Each layer is individually correct.
> The composition is wrong because no layer independently tests that its inputs are
> what it assumes."
>
> "**Trust-chain poisoning is not a bug. It is a class of bug.**"

T1/T2/T3 map onto L1/L2/L3; **T0 (distribution) is the addition** and sits upstream
of all of them.

**The control-surface substrate** is a four-stage pipeline — corpus →
setpoint-compression → committed rendition → deterministic playback — whose terminal
guarantee is *"no LLM, no template substitution, no network. Identical committed
rendition → byte-identical surface on every call."* The corpus behind this
repository's own `AGENTS.md` holds 367 entries tiered **232 invariant / 135
compressible**; invariant entries are emitted verbatim at every setpoint and are
never dropped or rewritten. That 0-Kelvin floor is a populated data field, not an
aspiration.

**Code architecture** is ports-and-adapters, stdlib-first, with Pydantic as the one
ratified exception. The rule is unusually specific about what it does *not* mean:
*"Domain cohesion lives in the type system, not the folder tree… `core/` stays; do
NOT add `domain`/`application`/`adapters`/`contexts` folder partitions to 'do DDD.'"*
The one place this is testable holds: `networkx` is imported in exactly one module,
behind a port returning `set[str]` rather than a library type.

## The claim each mechanism makes

Design choices carry claims. These are the load-bearing ones, each stated as what it
makes impossible or replaces:

| Mechanism | The claim | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Append-only ledger | Runtime truth is witnessed, not asserted; frontmatter cannot certify completion | **Engages** — 16,973 events, correction is a forward netting operation, never an edit |
| ADR → OBPI → REQ | Work decomposed until completion is *provable*; no untyped acceptance criterion | **Engages** — every REQ declares BEHAVIOR / SUPPORT / STRUCTURAL-FENCE with one proof channel |
| Five gates | Verification staged and non-skippable; *"a file or marker being present shows only that something is armed"* | **Partial** — lane→gate mapping is data, not code; but no ADR has reached closeout since 2026-07-31 |
| Human attestation | Cannot be automated or bypassed, in any lane, kind or sensitivity | **Engages hardest** — see below |
| Corpus → rendition | A hand-edited control surface is the same failure class as a hand-written ledger row | **Engages** — playback is byte-identical; drift fail-closes |
| Airlock | An un-declared seam makes GO structurally unreachable | **Vacuous** — see below |
| ARB receipts | Replaces agent narrative with wrapped-command receipts | **Engages** — 659 + 466 + 235 receipt events |
| Chore system | A chore is authorised to find, analyse, solve and fix on a cadence | **Newly mechanised** — 40 chores now carry declared class and rung |

## Which mechanisms engage

This is the question the baseline asked once, about the airlock, and did not
generalise. Generalised, it sorts gzkit's mechanisms into three states.

### Engages hardest: attestation, and the hooks

The universality of Gate 5 is not merely documented — the branch that could waive it
was **deleted**, and the function that would have decided it is now a constant:

```python
def _requires_human_obpi_attestation(parent_adr, parent_lane, brief_frontmatter=None) -> bool:
    """...human attestation is UNIVERSAL... The foundation/lane/security branching
    logic has been collapsed. The signature is preserved for call-site
    compatibility; all three parameters are accepted but not evaluated."""
    return True
```

Three parameters kept so call sites compile, all three ignored. A runtime invariant
check asserts it. This is the opposite of the vacuity pattern: the permissive branch
was removed rather than defaulted.

The hooks engage with equal force, and I can testify to it directly: **five times
during the production of this report a project hook refused one of my own shell
commands**, named the binding rule, quoted it, and printed the corrected command.
Four of the five were the verification exit-code integrity gate catching me piping a
verifier into a filter, which would have reported the filter's exit status instead of
the verifier's. That is enforcement observed rather than asserted.

Of 76 trust-audit modules, 63 can raise a blocking `ValidationError`; 9 carry an
advisory arm. The majority genuinely fail closed.

### Vacuous: built, tested, and structurally unable to fire

**The airlock.** Of 71 transits, all **53** `proceed` decisions computed an empty
seam-map and all **18** `hold`s a non-empty one. The correlation is total because
the decision function returns proceed exactly when the seam-map is falsy — so the
mechanism has never once evaluated a populated map and chosen to proceed. The cause
is wiring: `parent_invariants` is declared with default `()` and is passed by no
caller in `src/` — only by tests. Two further parameters on the same function,
`blast_radius: int = 0` and `override: CaptainOverride | None = None`, are likewise
passed by nothing, so one authority branch is dead in production. Two call sites
compute reach as a literal `lambda _node: []`. And the airlock ships
**diagnostic-only** by design — *"surfaced drift prints findings but still exits 0
— it reports, it never blocks."*

**Rendition lineage.** The grading gate for control-surface provenance reports
**0 of 22 sections owned, 0 of 19,872 bytes (0.0%)**, with 20 sections ungraded
because the lineage artifact does not exist — and exits 0. To the validator's
enormous credit it refuses to launder this: it says outright that counting those
sections as owned *"would claim proof this gate does not have,"* and forbids lowering
the declaration to clear the gate. The gate is well-built. Its input is empty.

**Validator reach.** Bare `gz validate` runs **15 of 97** scopes; 82 are flag-gated.
The project's own reachability chore classifies **51 of 99 runnable scopes as
ungated** — 27 invoked only by tests, 23 only by documentation, 1 by nothing at all.

**Declared vocabulary.** The ledger declares 77 event types; **10 have never fired.**

### The correction this report had to make about itself

An evidence pass for this report concluded that Gate 5 had lapsed — no `attested`
event since 2026-07-31 against subsequent OBPI completions. Checked directly, that
is **false**. `attested` is an *ADR-closeout* event; OBPI attestation is recorded
inside the completion receipt. All 7 completions since 2026-07-31 carry
`attestor: g0` and `obpi_completion: attested_completed`. Gate 5 held on every one.

The gap means only what the baseline already said: no ADR has reached closeout since
`0.34.0`. The near-miss is worth recording because it is the project's own named
failure mode — a consumer reading a field without independently verifying what the
producer means by it — arriving inside a report about that failure mode.

## The central uncertainty

Unchanged: **does this work for anyone not building it?** Flight tests remain 0 of 6
sorties flown; `rhea` remains stopped at 0 completed OBPIs; all issues ever opened
still have one author.

One thing the baseline left open can now be closed. It cited "574 downloads last
month" alongside "9 stars, 0 forks" without reconciling them, leaving open whether
the downloads were mirror traffic. They are not. The statistics API splits the two,
and the figure the baseline quoted is the **non-mirror** one: over the 30 days to
2026-09-19, **560 non-mirror downloads against 3,896 with mirrors**. So mirror
traffic is roughly 7× and is reported separately.

This does not establish human adopters — non-mirror downloads include CI and
container builds, and nothing here attributes a single install to a person. But the
cheapest available explanation for the download figure is now ruled out, and any
future month-over-month claim has to say which of the two series it is using.

## What would move this assessment

Observations that would change the reading, in either direction. Not
recommendations; work selection is the operator's.

**Would strengthen it materially:** one airlock transit computing a non-empty
seam-map from a real `parent_invariants` set — converting the membrane from designed
to operating. A committed lineage artifact moving rendition ownership off 0.0%. And,
unchanged from the baseline and still outranking both: one completed governed unit
of work in a repository that is not gzkit.

**Would weaken it:** a further quarter with `ADR-0.36.0` and `ADR-0.37.0` still at
zero landed OBPIs; the ungated-validator count rising rather than falling; or a new
mechanism shipping with the same unwired-default signature after this report names
the pattern.

**Untested either way:** whether the covenant's central value claim — that this
prevents an operator's slow slide into rubber-stamping — is true. No instrument in
the repository measures it. That remains the hardest measurement in the project and
the one nobody has solved.

---

## In summary

Read as a built system rather than as a health chart, gzkit is more impressive than
the baseline conveyed and its central weakness is more specific. The design is
coherent, the documents are honest about their own gaps, the distribution contract
is real, and the enforcement that does engage engages hard enough to refuse its own
author five times in one session.

Against that, four mechanisms are built and unwired, and they share one signature:
a declared parameter with a permissive default that nothing populates. The project's
witnesses confirm that mechanisms exist. Nothing yet asks whether they ever fire on
a non-empty input — which is why all four pass, and why the airlock could sit at
53-for-53 without any gate noticing.

### Material limitations

1. **This is not a trend report.** It publishes 25 hours after the baseline. Every
   trend claim it makes is inherited from the baseline's measurement window, not
   established by the interval between them.
2. **The PRD is a Draft from 2026-01 and is superseded in at least three places** —
   Gate 5 lane scoping (ADR-0.0.36 made it universal), the CLI framework, and command
   scope. It is cited here for its problem statement and origin only.
3. **The README's "all state lives in Markdown" is stale** against the
   L2-authoritative ledger that the state doctrine defines. Noted, not repaired.
4. **"Vacuous" is a claim about input wiring, not about code quality.** All four
   mechanisms named are well-constructed; three of them report their own gap
   honestly. The finding is that nothing detects the gap mechanically.
5. **The four-mechanism list is not exhaustive.** It is what four evidence passes
   surfaced; the reachability and inertness chores suggest the population is larger.
6. **One delegated finding was refuted, not merely corrected.** A claim that Gate 5
   had lapsed was checked against the receipts and found false. Other delegated
   figures were re-measured and held, but the episode bounds how much weight any
   un-re-measured delegated figure should carry — so none is load-bearing here.
7. **Adopter evidence remains partly unobservable.** Download counts cannot
   distinguish a person from a CI job, and no instrument here attributes an install
   to a human.
