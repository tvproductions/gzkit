## Bibliographic facts

- **Publisher:** IEEE (IEEE Standards Association).
- **Designation:** IEEE 1028-2008.
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Software Reviews and Audits.
- **Edition:** 2008 (board approval 2008-06-16, published 2008-08-15). It supersedes IEEE 1028-1998.
- **Status:** Inactive-Reserved, inactivated 2019-11-07. The IEEE SA page lists no successor. This research did not establish whether a revision project exists: a keyword search of standards.ieee.org surfaced none, which does not prove there is none.
- **Publisher URL:** <https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1028/4402/>

## Scope (paraphrased)

The standard defines five types of software review and audit, and the procedure for carrying out each one. It does not cover how to decide whether a review or audit is needed, or what to do with its results. Its procedures apply throughout the software life cycle.
