## Scope (paraphrased)

The book is a historical ethnography of the decision to launch Challenger; its appendix on method names that approach. The publisher presents it as showing that the launch was a mistake produced by an organizational culture of high-risk technology, not by misconduct. It also presents the book as explaining how NASA insiders, faced repeatedly with evidence of a problem, came to treat that evidence as acceptable. The new preface of the enlarged edition carries the analysis to the Columbia accident.
