## Scope (paraphrased)

Annex 19 gathers ICAO's overarching safety-management provisions in one place. It covers:

- the safety management responsibilities of States, including the State safety programme (SSP);
- safety management system (SMS) requirements for service providers;
- the collection, analysis, exchange and protection of safety data and safety information.

Amendment 1 did four things:

- merged the State safety oversight critical elements with the SSP provisions;
- raised the four SSP framework components to a Standard;
- extended the SMS to organizations that design and manufacture engines and propellers;
- strengthened the protection of safety data and safety information, and of their sources.

Amendment 2 does three things:

- strengthens SSP and SMS provisions;
- extends the SMS to operators of remotely piloted aircraft and to certified heliports;
- adds safety intelligence to support decision-making.
