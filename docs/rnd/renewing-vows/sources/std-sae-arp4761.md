- **Designation:** ARP4761 (Aerospace Recommended Practice), DOI 10.4271/ARP4761. Current revision: ARP4761A, DOI 10.4271/ARP4761A.
- **Title:** ARP4761 is Guidelines and Methods for Conducting the Safety Assessment Process on Civil Airborne Systems and Equipment. ARP4761A is retitled Guidelines for Conducting the Safety Assessment Process on Civil Aircraft, Systems, and Equipment. Its EUROCAE counterpart is ED-135.

## Scope (paraphrased)

**ARP4761 (1996).** It gives guidelines and methods for performing the safety assessment for certification of civil aircraft, mainly to show compliance with FAR/JAR 25.1309. It presents its methods as one systematic means of showing compliance, not the only one. It introduces the aircraft-level safety assessment and outlines the tools for carrying it out. For derivative or changed designs, the assessment normally covers only what the change affects, and service experience may serve as an alternative means.

**ARP4761A (2023), with ED-135.** It gives guidelines for safety assessments of civil aircraft, systems and equipment. They can be used for compliance across:

- 14 CFR and CS Parts 23, 25, 27 and 29;
- 14 CFR Parts 33 and 35;
- CS-E and CS-P.

They can also support a company's internal safety assessment standards. ARP4761A excludes in-service safety assessment, which ARP5150A and ARP5151A cover, and security threats.
