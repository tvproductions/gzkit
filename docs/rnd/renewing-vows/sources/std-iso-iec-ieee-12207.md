## Scope (paraphrased)

The standard sets out a common process framework for the whole life of a software system, from conception to retirement. It covers acquisition and supply as well as development, operation, maintenance and disposal, whether the work is done inside or outside the organization. Its processes can be applied concurrently, iteratively and recursively, and users select from them to build life cycle models suited to their projects. It is aligned with ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 for system life cycle processes.

The 2026 foreword lists the main changes:

- updates to several technical processes, including business or mission analysis, architecture definition, implementation, integration, operation and maintenance;
- improvements to technical management processes, including risk management and configuration management;
- revised key concepts: iteration, recursion, system of systems and quality characteristics;
- new material on concept and system definition, and expanded material on agile methods;
- a revised annex on model-based systems and software engineering.
