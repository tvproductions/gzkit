# Standards and Book Sources Index

Research date: 2026-10-06. Persona: main-session — craftsperson, governance-aware, whole-file-reasoning, direct. Provenance: R&D run `renewing-vows`. This is an index of the citation records for the copyrighted standards and books, plus one EU regulation, cited by the R&D record. Each record cites and paraphrases its source and does not copy it. Public-domain US-government sources are indexed separately in `README-us-gov.md`.

Verdicts: VERIFIED means public publisher material supports the claim. NOT VERIFIED means the claim rests on text that is not publicly inspectable. CONTRADICTED means public material says otherwise.

## Retrieval conditions on 2026-10-06

- **iso.org.** It returned HTTP 403 to automated requests and showed a human-verification challenge in a browser. The challenge was not completed. The four ISO/IEC/IEEE joint standards were therefore read from two other sources: the IEEE SA pages of IEEE, the co-publisher, and the official sample pages served by iTeh Standards (publisher front matter, contents and opening clauses). ISO-side status comes from the search-engine index of iso.org and is labelled that way in each record.
- **sae.org.** Product pages rendered no content, either in a browser or to automated fetch. Every SAE record was read from SAE MOBILUS (saemobilus.sae.org), SAE's own platform.
- **RTCA.** The public product pages give only title, date, committee and description. The structural claims about DO-178C and DO-330 were checked against FAS Topic Papers hosted by RTCA. Those papers state that they are informational and are not RTCA/EUROCAE policy, and every use is labelled.
- **IEEE 1012.** The IV&V definition was checked in SEVOCAB, the vocabulary database run jointly by the IEEE Computer Society and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 7. SEVOCAB cites IEEE 1012-2024, clause 3.1.
- **EASA.** The EUR-Lex consolidated text (07.08.2026) was read at the CRS provisions. The header of `std-easa-part-145.md` departs from the standard header sentence, because EU legal text may be excerpted. Only 145.A.50(a)–(b) and M.A.801(e) are excerpted.
- **Vaughan.** The publisher lists only the 2016 Enlarged Edition. The 1996 date comes from a secondary catalog record and is marked NOT VERIFIED.
- **Manning.** The product page and the liveBook free extract were read only for the MEAP version, the chapter list and the chapter 4 overview.

2. ISO/IEC/IEEE 14764:2022 defines five maintenance types, not four. Additive maintenance is the new type, distinct from perfective.

## Load-bearing claims that remain NOT VERIFIED

- **29148, ConOps ordering.** No public text puts the ConOps/OpsCon above the StRS. The public structure places operational-concept content inside the BRS (9.3.16) and the StRS (9.4.16), and the ConOps annex is informative.
- **ARP4754A, DALs from failure-condition severity.** This is the premise of the lane-follows-severity doctrine. No public SAE text states the severity-to-level mapping.
- **EIA-649C, CM function names.** The public scope gives only the count (five) and the location (Section 5). The names were corroborated from the companion EIA649_1A page, which is a different document.
- **DO-178C detail.** Level E, the statement, decision and MC/DC criteria by level, bidirectional traceability, and § 11.17 are all in the purchased text only.
- **Krishnan § 4.2.5 heading.** It can be cited only from the licensed copy, with the MEAP version stated.
