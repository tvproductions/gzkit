## Scope (paraphrased)

MSG-3 is a decision-logic process for setting the initial scheduled maintenance requirements of new aircraft and/or power plants: fixed-wing in Volume 1, rotorcraft in Volume 2. It is a way of developing maintenance tasks and intervals that regulators, operators and manufacturers will accept. The 2022.1 product page lists the issue papers incorporated in that revision.
