# Vocabulary matrix — maintenance terms for the hangar and drydock

Prepared 2026-10-25 for the R&D run `docs/rnd/contractor-kit.md`. Operator, verbatim, ordering it: *"do a deep dive, and come up with a matrix that favors the more formal naval/aviation terms - the literature on them, even if we are taking liberties here, would be richer than our vibing."*

**What this is.** A view drafted by the agent. It holds no authority and rules nothing. The scoped-register ruling (*"D, yes."*) says the maintenance vocabulary governs inside the hangar and drydock, Part 0 governs the force, and one recorded crosswalk binds them row by row, each contested row ruled singly. This matrix is the input to those rulings.

**How each cell was checked.** Every quotation in a cell was checked by the agent against its source after the four inventories were verified (`sources/verify-inventory-*.md`). The sources are the `pdftotext -layout` extractions of the TUM and JFMM PDFs, the repository source files for the aviation and maritime texts, and the gzkit canon files named. Where an inventory cut a condition, the cell restores it. The check script and its result are recorded at the end.

**Preference order for a proposal**, as ordered: the Navy texts (TUM, JFMM), then 14 CFR and the FAA circulars, then DFARS, FAR, OSHA and 46 CFR. Next comes a term gzkit canon has already ruled, and last the operator's own word.

**Standing.**

- **ruled**: the operator has ruled the term (cited).
- **held**: recorded in the run as a term, but not yet tested against the Navy texts.
- **proposed**: the agent recommends the term; it is sourced and not yet ruled.
- **contested**: the sources disagree with each other or with canon, and an operator ruling is needed.
- **liberty**: the term is taken with a departure from its source, and the departure is stated.
- **gap**: there is no source term, or no gzkit position.

**Source keys.**

- TUM — NAVSEA S0400-AD-URM-010, Tag-Out User's Manual, Rev 08, 28 Oct 2020.
- JFMM — COMUSFLTFORCOMINST 4790.3 Rev D Chg 5, Volume V, 13 Jun 2025. Citation `I-2.2.3` = Part I ¶ 2.2.3; *gl.* = the Part I glossary.
- 14 CFR, AC — eCFR 2026-10-15; AC 120-16G (1/4/16), AC 43-9D, AC 00-71.
- DFARS, FAR, 29 CFR, 46 CFR — eCFR as fetched 2026-10-25.
