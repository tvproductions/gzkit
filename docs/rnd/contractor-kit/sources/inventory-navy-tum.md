- **Edition:** Revision 08, 28 OCT 2020. The title page says: "THIS REVISION SUPERSEDES REVISION 07 ACN 1/A OF 12 JUL 2016 ACN 2/A OF 20 DEC 2018". Published "by direction of Commander, Naval Sea Systems Command".
- **Text used:** `pdftotext -layout` extraction (scratchpad `tum.txt`, 4,521 lines). Quotes are copied from that extraction. Where the PDF breaks a sentence across lines, the lines are joined with one space. Source spelling and typos are kept as printed (for example "positon" in 1.6.4.a(6)(c), "Principle Assistant" in 1.7.4.c(3), "4.dc.(1)" in App F 4.d, "CONDIDTION" in App I 3.e). Section numbers are the manual's own.
- **Read in full:** the certification sheet change list (pp. 1–6 of the sheet); References (a)–(m); Chapter 1 complete: 1.1 Purpose, 1.1.1, 1.2 Applicability, 1.3 Responsibilities (1.3.1 Ship's Force, 1.3.2 Authorizing Officer, 1.3.3 Repair Activity), 1.4 Training and Qualifications, 1.5 Planning (1.5.1 Logs and Records, 1.5.2 Use of Line Items and Tags), 1.6 Establishing (1.6.1 Creating, 1.6.2 Independent Reviews, 1.6.3 Authorization, 1.6.4 Posting, 1.6.5 Check of Posted Tags, 1.6.6 Beginning Work, 1.6.7 Iterative, 1.6.8 Certified Line Items), 1.7 Maintaining (1.7.1–1.7.6), 1.8 Clearing (1.8.1–1.8.7), 1.9 Validating the Database. Appendices read in full: A (Acronyms), B (Glossary), C (Repair Activity Tag-outs), F (Tag-out Standards), H (Amplifications for the Manual for the Control of Testing and Plant Conditions), I (Electronic Tag-out Program Administration), J (Master Tag-outs), K (Manual Tag-out System, including the block-by-block overviews of the Index Sheet, the TORS front and back, and the Danger and Caution tags).
- **Read in part:** Appendix D (Tag-out and Calibration Forms). Read: the LIRS, THS and TRS figures with their block-by-block overviews, the Danger Tag overview, the Instrument Log overview, and the Audit Report Cover Sheet and Audit Report (Figures 10–11). Skimmed only: the Caution Tag example and the OOC/CAL label figures (Figures 5–9). These are form layouts that repeat the main body.
- **Skipped, with reason:** Appendix E (Divers), Appendix G (Barrier Criteria) and Appendix L (OOC/CAL Labels). These cover domain-specific engineering for diving, sea and pressure barriers, and instrument labels. They add no roles or records to the general regime beyond those already listed. I grepped them for "independen", "remov", "stop" and "authoriz"; the only role-bearing hits were in Appendix H, which I then read in full. The TMDER form at the end was also skipped.

**[reading]** In Chapter 1 and the appendices I read, no rule requires the person who posted a tag to be the one who removes it. Posting goes to "a person" the AO assigns (1.6.3.d(4)); removal goes to "The person assigned" (1.8.5.b). Removal authority sits with the AO and, where applicable, the RA Representative, who co-sign the TRS. It does not sit with the applier. This is the inverse of personal lock-out (one lock, one key, held by the worker). The control here is administrative: a log-held authorization and a signed sheet. This is a reading of the passages quoted above. The manual does not state the contrast itself.
