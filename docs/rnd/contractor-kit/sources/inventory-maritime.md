### Readings (interpretation, not quotation)

- **[reading]** The texts give two different "inspector" patterns. Under the contract clauses, the repairer runs its own inspection system and the owner (Government) checks it "at any time" (252.217-7005(c), (c)(3)). Under the hot-work rule, a credentialed outsider (marine chemist) certifies before work begins, and the regulator (OCMI) chooses a substitute when none is available (46 CFR 71.60-1(c)(1)). Only 1915.89(q)(2) states an independence condition in so many words, and it applies to the auditor.
- **[reading]** The restoration step is split. The repairer's authorized employee removes the lock and starts up (1915.89(i), (j)). The proving run (dock and sea trials) is under "the vessel's commander and crew" (252.217-7010(d)(1)). Acceptance is a separate Government act (252.217-7010(b); FAR 46.101).
- **[reading]** "Job order" is the contract and "master agreement" is explicitly "not a contract" (217.7101(a)(2)). The standing terms cannot be changed through a job order (217.7103-6(b)).
- **[reading]** The texts read never use the words "restore", "restorer" or "return to service" as a role. The nearest phrases are "restoring the machinery, equipment, or system to use" (1915.89(i)(1)) and "ready to return to normal operating condition" (1915.89 App. A, non-mandatory).
