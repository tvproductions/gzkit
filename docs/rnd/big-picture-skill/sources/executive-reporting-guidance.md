# Executive reporting guidance

Read 2026-09-19 during the big-picture-skill R&D run. Researcher persona:
main-session. External guidance is research material, not operator authority.

## Primary source · IFRS Foundation integrated reporting FAQs

Source: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/integrated-reporting/faqs/

> qualitative and quantitative disclosures, because each provides context for the other

The FAQ connects purpose, strategy, performance, dependencies and prospects through value
over time. It distinguishes combining existing reports from explaining relationships
among their contents. It supports project-specific measures and says value need not be
converted into a financial valuation. It also acknowledges uncertainty in interpreting
outcomes. This is corporate reporting guidance; adopting useful principles does not make
the proposed project report compliant with that framework.

## Primary source · Financial Reporting Council materiality guidance

Source: https://www.frc.org.uk/library/research-and-insights/materiality/embed-a-materiality-mindset/

> Are underperforming businesses and adverse events given sufficient prominence compared to success stories?

The guidance recommends reviewing the report as a whole, removing duplication, reconsidering
rolled-forward content, and checking whether important messages or adverse developments
are obscured. Its relevance here is narrative balance and coherence, not financial compliance.

## Research interpretation · implications for this skill

These are proposals for operator consideration, not source quotations or settled rulings:

- Organize around project purpose and value, with evidence explaining the connections.
- Explain what work enabled, preserved, or made harder; raw activity cannot establish this.
- Give quantitative evidence context through qualitative interpretation.
- Select what materially changes understanding of the whole project; do not concatenate
  all existing status reports.
- Treat architectural judgments as interpretations whose supporting and contrary evidence
  can be inspected. A compelling story is insufficient proof of causation.
- Keep the project's own vocabulary and audience. Do not import corporate reporting
  obligations, capital categories, financial estimates, or an investor-relations tone.

## Proposed evaluation question

Could an operator explain what this project is becoming, why the recent work matters,
and which major architectural uncertainties remain after reading the report? This tests
the requested perspective; section completion alone does not.
