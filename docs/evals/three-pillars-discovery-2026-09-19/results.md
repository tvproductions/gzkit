# Historical discovery pilot — results

Dated 2026-09-19. Persona: main-session. Work order:
[GHI #1045](https://github.com/tvproductions/gzkit/issues/1045).
Current-code reference for this investigation: `7bc931575`.

## Disposition

**Do not adopt the impact-account instruction as a canonical rule.** Six fresh
historical investigations do not establish a reliable improvement. Both arms
found important producer/consumer relationships, but both proposed an unbooked
reversal of the sync guard's explicit inspection-error policy. One candidate
run also breached the snapshot-only boundary by listing the current checkout's
persona directory. The frozen disposition rule rejects adoption on a protocol
breach independently of the criterion totals.

This completes the commissioned pilot; it does not complete general semantic
dependency discovery. No runtime code, canonical instruction, proof-currency
policy, ADR or OBPI was changed by this experiment. A null result is a reason
to withhold an unsupported remedy, not to manufacture another implementation.

## What was measured

Three complaint-only historical tasks, two conditions each, one fresh agent per
cell. Both conditions had the same case snapshot and resource limits. The
candidate alone received a request to account for changed meaning, producers,
representations, consumers, obligations and uncertainty before recommending a
repair. The [protocol](protocol.md), [cases](cases.json) and exact
[inputs](inputs.json) were frozen before dispatch. [Curation](curation.md)
records the independent oracle challenge and pre-dispatch corrections.

All six answers are retained verbatim as R01–R06. A separate reviewer received
response labels and case identities without the condition mapping; treatment
could still be inferred from the writing. Scores concern expressed discovery,
reasoning and proposed application, not executed patches or actual regression
rates. Criteria describe a minimum expected population, not exhaustive scope.

That one-point difference is **borderline, not robust comparative evidence**.
R02 does not quote the governance sentence either; the reviewer credits its
named document, explicit test and targeted freshness amendment together. A
stricter reading removes the candidate point and yields 16–16. The primary

reporting this sensitivity. R05's C4 diagnostic repair also receives credit
from its explicit false-blame diagnosis plus conditional diagnostic proposal;
it is less explicit than neutral mismatch wording. Neither ambiguity changes
the no-adoption disposition.

Both H answers discover the budget and advise protecting lineage under overflow,
but omit explicit preservation of freshness warnings in that overflow control
(H5 A=0). This is missing demonstrated coverage, not an observed regression.
Both S answers achieve six complete minimum criteria **and** carry two material
scope flags: reversing fail-open inspection policy and expanding admission to
the no-auto-add/final-recheck path. A perfect minimum-coverage score therefore
does not certify a safe bounded recommendation. These flags were recorded
separately, as the protocol required, rather than retroactively adding criteria.

Both S answers also propose verified generated-package exceptions. The reviewer
checked the historical bundled surface-sync attribution rule and accepted that
as a defensible design alternative, while retaining its added implementation
burden and unresolved edge cases. Both H answers propose richer ancestor-body
projection and orientation coverage; these plausibly address the complaint but
are not mandatory oracle items. No score demands matching the historical patch.
The reviewer found no claim that a proposed repair had actually been executed.

## Implications for the three pillars

1. **Usable context:** within these short investigations, a named budget and
   accessible files did not ensure every constraint was applied. This does not
   test long-session retention, attention scaling or context-window capacity.
2. **Relationship reasoning:** both conditions traced real producers and
   consumers, including the distinction between valid Task syntax and semantic
   ownership. Finding those connections did not reliably bound the remedy.
3. **Discovery:** the tasks withheld the later patches and affected-file lists.
   This improves on the previous explicitly cued decision exercise, but the
   selected snapshots still contain clues. Neither result measures recall over
   all unknown obligations, and an impact account is not a dependency oracle.

The actionable adjustment is to retain separate evidence for finding a surface,
understanding its obligation and honoring it in the proposed change. This is
an interpretation of the evaluation, not a new mandatory workflow. In
particular, do not narrow proof currency on an agent's self-selected impact list.

## Current-code reconciliation and remaining boundaries

These are historical failures, not newly discovered current defects. The
acceptance case's old repair omitted its contrary governance prose, but reading
current `docs/governance/acceptance-obligations.md` and the identity producer
confirmed that the current documentation already distinguishes ambient
environment from artifact identity. No duplicate repair was opened.

Earlier handoff and skill-audit repairs remain described in the
[recovered assessment](../three-pillars-2026-09-19/assessment.md). This pilot
neither replaces their regression evidence nor expands their completeness claim.
GHI #1028 remains production observation, #1029 the proof-currency design, and
#943 adjacent model-profile calibration; all three were verified open on
2026-09-19. No synthetic OBPI was used to manufacture production evidence.

Before any broader claim, a further authorized study would need a held-out case
set, repeated runs, stronger access isolation and actual patch verification.
That is a limit on inference, not a newly initiated study or a prerequisite
invented for closing this bounded investigation.
