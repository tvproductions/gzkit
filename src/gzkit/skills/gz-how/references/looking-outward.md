# Looking outward

You are here when something outside the project — another tool, a sibling
repository, a pasted idea — might hold a lesson.

## Steps

- **An open problem, outcome unknown** → **`gz-rnd`** (operator-invoked): a
  governed R&D session that defines the problem and ends in a disposition map.
  A need the operator has already ruled is design work instead; see
  [Idea to ADR](idea-to-adr.md).
- **gzkit repository only** — the competitor radar and the AirlineOps parity
  scan are listed in the hub catalog under Looking outward; they are not
  delivered to adopters.

## Branches

- **The lesson is already clear** → [Idea to ADR](idea-to-adr.md).
- **It is a defect the outside view exposed** → [Defects and GHIs](defects-and-ghis.md).

## Only you can

Start an R&D session, and sign off its disposition map.

## Then

The flow the disposition map names.
