  new pool ADR or new-design ceremony. **Rationale:** operator "correction vs
  enhancement" doctrine — the gaps meant the surface did not yet fulfill its
  declared intent (e.g. BI#1's "graph never lies about the shape"). **Alternatives
  rejected:** fresh pool ADR; treating the gaps as enhancements.
- **Decision:** Registry-coupled rebuild-fidelity fence (BI#1 + OBPI-02
  REQ-02-05). **Rationale:** the prior self-report could silently drop a future
  event type; diffing against the live `TypedLedgerEvent` union closes the
  laundered-blind-spot. **Alternatives rejected:** a hardcoded handled-type set
  (that was the defect).
- **Decision:** OBPI-06 gets a mechanical fail-closed emission gate (kind
  SUPPORT → BEHAVIOR); it is NOT split into its own ADR. **Rationale:** the gate
  is the less-invasive correction and preserves the GO-attested single-ADR shape.
  **Alternatives rejected:** splitting OBPI-06 out.
- **Decision:** tree-sitter re-justified and kept. **Rationale:** reversing or
  deferring it repeats the GO-attested substrate drift; polyglot adopter-codebase
  parsing is a named-capability gap `ast` cannot fill. **Alternatives rejected:**
  defer/remove tree-sitter until a non-Python adopter exists.
- **Decision:** OBPI-04 not folded. **Rationale:** folding would break the 1:1
  ADR-checklist ↔ OBPI-brief sync mandate. **Alternatives rejected:** fold into
  OBPI-01/02 closeout.
- **Decision:** `SUBSTANCE_REVIEW.md` written as a separate artifact; the
  structural `EVALUATION_SCORECARD.md` left intact. **Rationale:** GHI #624 —
  substance and structural scores must never be composited. **Alternatives
