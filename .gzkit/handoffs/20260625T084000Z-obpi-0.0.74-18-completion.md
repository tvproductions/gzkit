## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Fix the claim-binding defect inside OBPI-18 (this pipeline),
  not as a separate GHI/ceremony.
  **Rationale:** The Step-4b adversary caught it pre-attestation; that is the
  pipeline's own Stage-2 recovery path. Operator pushed "why not straight fix?".
  **Alternatives rejected:** A new GHI (over-ceremony for in-flight work); shipping
  the registry-global check as "done" (fails REQ-18-01's "for that claim").

- **Decision:** Defer the `closeout_proof.py` consumer wiring to OBPI-0.0.74-19.
  **Rationale:** Wiring the 2-arg consumer to pass `req_text` now would flip
  meta-property fences (REQ-15-04/16-06/17-05, which name no claim) to
  `unproven-fence` and fail-close the floor before OBPI-19 completes claim
  coverage — violating ADR-0.0.74 §5 strict-no-debt sequencing. Operator ratified
  ("if defect 2 is slated for obpi-19, then it is scheduled").
  **Alternatives rejected:** Wiring `closeout_proof.py` here (breaks meta-property
  fence closeouts, denied path); leaving the coupling untracked (untrackable defect
  = nonexistent defect).

- **Decision:** Resolver "live NC" means "the named claim is registered", not "the
  NC passes when run".
  **Rationale:** Run-it-to-verify liveness is the meta-validator floor (OBPI-19,
  the ADR's deferred perf/cadence decision). OBPI-18 owns the cheap resolver-layer
  presence check.
  **Alternatives rejected:** Running `run_meta_validator` inside `resolve_fence_proof`
  (heavy; does OBPI-19's job).
