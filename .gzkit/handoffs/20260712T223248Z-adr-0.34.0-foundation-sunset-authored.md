## Decisions Made

The design was booked across five operator-approved sections plus a
forcing-function review that surfaced three would-be production failures and
their fixes. Key decisions and rejected alternatives:

- **Partition on Layer-2 ledger, three classes** (not frontmatter, not two
  classes). Rejected: partitioning on ADR-lifecycle Validated/Completed only
  (strands 5 Class-2 ADRs holding attested work); a blanket "pending → pool"
  migration (pools foundations holding attested work).
- **Committed grandfather manifest, identity-only, golden-file-guarded.** Rejected:
  computing the grandfathered set from on-disk (no teeth — a new ADR is silently
  grandfathered); storing lifecycle in the manifest (bakes Layer-2 truth into a
  committed Layer-1 file — the state-doctrine drift the 0.0.37 frontmatter-lie
  demonstrated). Manifest tamper-guard is a golden-file test (operator ruling),
  not a heavier ledger-witnessed seal.
- **Backfill-at-populate with attested `foundation_grandfathered`** (operator
  ruling). Rejected: a pure-ledger gate with no backfill (false-fires
  `foundation_limbo` on half of gzkit's pre-ledger history).
- **Ontology re-sense is a Sunset step** (operator ruling) — the 23-node
  `ADR-0.0.X`→`ADR-pool.slug` rename could orphan the corpus subgraph.
- **Class-2 finish-then-freeze** (operator ruling) — 0.0.37's 4 fabricated OBPIs
  are *withdrawn* (not rebuilt — rebuilding is net-new foundation work under a
  no-new-foundation regime), its real composition re-homed as a feature (GHI #623);
  0.0.65/0.0.72 are *finished* (their designed OBPIs built) as the sunset completes
  its members before locking.
- **5-way split** (operator ruling) over the original 4 — OBPI-04 was an overloaded
  mega-OBPI (independence=1); split into migration (-04) + activation (-05).
