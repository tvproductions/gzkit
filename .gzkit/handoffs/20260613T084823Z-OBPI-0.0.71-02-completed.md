## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Auto-derive `repudiated_receipt` from the ledger rather than a CLI flag.
  **Rationale:** The brief's REQs never list it; the most-recent completion receipt
  is deterministic. Cleaner operator UX.
  **Alternatives rejected:** `--repudiated-receipt` flag (unnecessary operator typing).

- **Decision:** Place `obpi_repudiate_cmd` in `obpi_cmd.py` (not a new module).
  **Rationale:** The brief names `obpi_cmd.py`; co-located with `obpi_withdraw_cmd`.
  **Alternatives rejected:** Separate `obpi_repudiate_cmd.py` (violates brief allowlist).

- **Decision:** Behave-waive REQ-04/06/07; tag REQ-01/02/03/05 in scenarios.
  **Rationale:** REQ-04's no-write assertion needs a completed-OBPI ledger fixture
  (unit-tested with mocks); 06 is structural-fence; 07 is support — none is a
  behave-appropriate BEHAVIOR REQ.
  **Alternatives rejected:** Standing up a completed-OBPI behave fixture (integration
  belongs at ADR-0.0.71 closeout).

- **Decision:** Accept the security floor via `--accept-security-floor`.
  **Rationale:** Operator-authorized; the repudiate verb is purely additive and
  structurally defensive (operator-gated, fail-closed, ledger-witnessed) and handles
  no credentials/subprocess/crypto.
  **Alternatives rejected:** Declaring `sensitivity: security` and blocking until the
  scanner toolchain lands (would strand a defensive feature indefinitely).
