## Important Context

**Reconcile engine bug details:**
- File: `src/gzkit/governance/brief_reconcile.py`
- Line: `_REQ_LINE_RE = re.compile(r"REQUIREMENT:")`
- Bug: Looks for literal "REQUIREMENT:" but the brief format is "REQUIREMENT [BEHAVIOR]:" and "REQUIREMENT [SUPPORT]:" — the colon comes after the kind tag, not directly after "REQUIREMENT"
- Fix: Change regex to match both forms, e.g. `re.compile(r"REQUIREMENT\s*(?:\[\w+\])?\s*:")` or simply `re.compile(r"REQUIREMENT\s")`

**Pipeline gate block:** `src/gzkit/pipeline_runtime.py:check_reconcile_receipt_gate` blocks Stage 2 entry when `has_drift=True`, including false-positive drifts from the engine bug.
