## Important Context

- The guards READ the ledger (`gzkit.ledger.Ledger.query`) and marker
  (`marker.is_active`) only — they never mutate the ledger writer (brief Denied
  Paths). `_open_sessions` is the shared reader: `mx_session_opened` with no
  matching `mx_session_closed`.
- The honest mechanism for "must bite while the marker is up" is emitting
  CRITICAL — the checkpoint pins CRITICAL through an active marker and demotes
  every non-CRITICAL non-floor level to ADVISORY (`checkpoint.py:31`). This is
  why max-open and no-release emit CRITICAL while TTL/debt emit ERROR (advisory
  inside the hangar, grounding outside it).
- REQ-14-05 is `[structural-fence]`; its proof channel is parent-ADR
  `## Boundary Invariants #2`, NOT a `@covers` test. `gz covers` reports it
  `proof_status: pass` via PARENT_ADR_INVARIANT with `covered: false` — this is
  correct, not a gap (structural fences are @covers-exempt per tests.md).
