## Important Context

THE RULING THAT UNBLOCKED THIS OBPI. The brief as authored defined its unit twice
and incompatibly: REQ-05 raises the floor "by that section's byte count" (a span),
while REQ-02/07 quoted 22,378 unowned B and 31.2% coverage of 31,990 B — figures
computed as (document bytes - corpus-entry TEXT bytes), which is not a span and was
never distributed across the 14 sections REQ-07 attributed it to. Re-measured at the
authoring commit 4738aa69, the 14 unowned sections spanned 11,607 B, not 22,378 B.
The operator ruled span-based. The brief carries an AMENDED 2026-09-02 block.

BASELINE IS RE-DERIVED, NEVER STORED. Measured 2026-09-02: AGENTS.md is 46,876 B
across 22 H1/H2 sections; 10 corpus-addressed sections span 38,239 B; 12 unowned
sections span 8,637 B; coverage 81.6%. These figures are ILLUSTRATIVE. REQ-07 says
"derived by measurement, never by a stored constant", and a fix cycle already had to
strip literal pins of exactly these numbers out of the test suite because they
reproduce, one layer down, the same drift that made the brief stale.

SPAN-BASED INFLATES THE HEADLINE. An owned section's FULL span counts even where a
single corpus entry backs it. Four of the ten owned sections carry exactly one entry,
and `governance-doctrine-surfaces`'s single entry is `compressible` tier so it is not
on the invariant floor at all. 81.6% is six real sections plus four tokens. REQ-08's
honesty clause therefore binds compute_baseline's OUTPUT: it must surface the
per-section entry histogram alongside the percentage.

REUSE, DO NOT REINVENT. `gzkit.content.parse.section_id` is the canonical section-id
vocabulary ("a second slugifier would let those surfaces disagree"). Ownership keys on
it. A local slugifier silently fails REQ-06.

THE COLLISION GUARD. `measure_section_spans` fails closed when ANY second heading
boundary resolves to an already-seen section id — distinct titles or identical ones.
It initially guarded only distinct titles, which fixed the instance not the class.
Do not bypass it or catch OwnershipLoadError to keep going. One heading whose title
changed but whose id did not is a SINGLE boundary and must still resolve (REQ-06).

REQ-08 IS [support], NOT [behavior]. Its proof channel is an `artifact_edited` ledger
event citing `.gzkit/ownership/AGENTS.md.json` plus `gz validate --documents`. Never
author a unit test to make it look covered — ADR-0.0.59 names that category error and
GHI #571 is the instance.

STALE BYTECODE HAZARD, OBSERVED THIS SESSION. A stale __pycache__ produced a test
failure (`1 != 2`) whose message contradicted freshly-read source: the executed
bytecode was pre-edit while the traceback line text came from current disk. It looked
