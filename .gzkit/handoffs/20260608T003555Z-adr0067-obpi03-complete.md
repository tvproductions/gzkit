## Important Context

- **Sequencing is load-bearing.** OBPI-03 (this session) deleted the 3 `obpi
  lock-*` hyphen aliases. While the skill-alignment audit was top-level-only,
  those aliases were invisible to it. OBPI-01's recursion (`_known_cli_verb_paths`)
  is what closes that visibility gap — so it MUST land after the deletions, not
  before, or the audit would have flagged the aliases mid-flight.
- **Coupled-surface lesson from this session:** deleting a CLI verb touches more
  than the parser. OBPI-03 also had to remove the verb's manpage, its
  `doc-coverage.json` entries, its behave scenario, AND its `_NO_SKILL_VERBS`
  stale waiver in `src/gzkit/governance/trust_audits/cli.py`. The stale waiver
  was missed initially and broke 3 skill-alignment tests at full-suite scope —
  caught only by auditing ARB receipt exit codes (the first sweeps were piped
  through `| tail`, which masked the non-zero unittest exit behind tail's exit 0).
  **When verifying a full suite, never trust a `| tail`-piped exit code — read
  the ARB receipt's `exit_status` or capture stdout/stderr to a file.**
- **OBPI-01 verification surfaces** (already green this session, should stay green):
  `tests/governance/test_promoted_advisory_audits.py::PromotedAdvisoryAudits.test_skill_alignment_invariant_1`
  and `test_skill_alignment_enumerates_multiword_subcommands`, plus
  `tests/commands/test_skill_alignment_10verbs.py`.
- **REQ-kind discipline:** SUPPORT-kind REQs (doc/ledger-proved) must NOT get
  cosmetic `@covers` decorators; behave gaps for them use
  `data/behave_coverage_waivers.json` waivers with operator authorization.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Deleted `docs/user/manpages/obpi-lock-status.md` rather than
  keeping it (the plan-audit initially over-corrected to "keep all 3 manpages").
  **Rationale:** It was a redundant orphan — its content duplicated the canonical
  `obpi-lock-list.md` (which covers `gz obpi lock list`) under the dead-alias
  filename. `obpi-lock-claim.md` and `obpi-lock-release.md` ARE canonical and
  were kept. **Alternatives rejected:** Renaming status.md → list.md (rejected —
  list.md already exists); keeping status.md (rejected — orphan fails cli audit).
- **Decision:** Removed the 3 `_NO_SKILL_VERBS` waivers in `trust_audits/cli.py`
  and added that file to the brief allowlist. **Rationale:** Coupled-surface
  coherence — a waiver naming an unregistered verb fails the stale-waiver check
  (ADR § Decision (1) named this). **Alternatives rejected:** Leaving the waivers
  (rejected — breaks 3 tests).
- **Decision:** Registered an operator-authorized behave-coverage waiver for
  REQ-03-01/02/03. **Rationale:** REQ-01's positive half is behave-covered by
  existing scenarios and its negative half is unit-tested; REQ-02/03 are SUPPORT
  with no behave channel. **Alternatives rejected:** Authoring a behave scenario
  for the deleted verb (rejected — re-introduces a dead `gz obpi lock-claim`
  command-shape the operator-doc-verb-resolution validator rejects).
