Worse, `parse_support_citation` (`src/gzkit/req_kind_support.py:114`) extracts proof event types by plain substring scan: `found_types = [et for et in _KNOWN_LEDGER_EVENT_TYPES if et in req_text]`. Demonstrated live this session — a REQ sentence reading "The ledger carries NO corpus_entry_retired event for these ids — measured 0 of 8" parses as `event_types=['corpus_entry_retired']`. The parser cannot distinguish an assertion from its negation, so these REQs would resolve GREEN at closeout for precisely the wrong reason.

That is the presence-check-for-proof substitution this OBPI exists to remove, reproduced inside its own repair. It is why the hold is correct rather than pedantic.

The remedy converges with work already on record: a validator walking each `retires` pointer is arm 1 of GHI #885's blocker comment. It lives in `src/**`, a Denied Path for this OBPI, so it cannot land here.
